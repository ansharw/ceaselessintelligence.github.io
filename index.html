Problems We Evaluate

Growth Is Often Held Back Not by the Product, but by the System

Here are some of the problems we'll evaluate together—not an assumption that all of them are happening at your company.

  • New prospects still depend on referrals.
  • The sales team doesn't have a structured target list.
  • Follow-up stops after the quotation is sent.
  • Inquiries are scattered across email, phone, and WhatsApp.
  • Prospect data isn't recorded consistently.
  • Salespeople spend too much time on administrative work.
  • Reports are still created manually.
  • The same questions get answered over and over.
  • Internal information is hard to find.
  • There's no clear process for turning inquiries into opportunities.

We help fix the growth side first, then explore whether the operational side can also be improved.

AI Solutions

AI That Follows Business Needs—Not the Other Way Around

We don't add technology just to make a business look modern.

Before making a recommendation, we study:

  • Who performs the work
  • How often the process happens
  • How long the process takes
  • What data and applications are used
  • What the risk of error is
  • Whether automation actually delivers a benefit

A process is worth automating when it

  • Happens repeatedly
  • Has fairly clear rules
  • Requires a lot of copy-paste or data entry
  • Causes delays
  • Uses data that can be accessed securely
  • Delivers a benefit greater than its implementation cost

Clients can receive

  • Workflow map
  • Bottleneck analysis
  • AI opportunity map
  • Solution recommendation
  • Implementation scope
  • Cost and timeline proposal
  • Testing and quality assurance
  • Documentation and team handover
